## 📂 Featured Projects

### ✈️ Spanish Outbound Tourism — Can online searches anticipate travel demand?
End-to-end pipeline integrating **INE mobile-phone mobility data, DataForSEO search volumes and Google Trends** into a **1.5M-row dataset** (2019–2025, 45 destinations). Web scraping, semantic keyword mapping, lag-correlation analysis and an interactive dashboard.
**Result:** search signals work as real-time monitors, not as 11-month forecasts — only 6 of 45 destinations keep a useful signal at the planning horizon.
`Python` `Pandas` `APIs` `Selenium` `SciPy` `Plotly` `Streamlit`
🔗 [Repository](https://github.com/vbujaldon/spain_outbound_tourism_analysis) · 🚀 [Live dashboard](https://spain-outbound-tourism-analysis-2026.streamlit.app/)

### 📊 HR Risk & Training Analytics — Statistical analysis for business decisions
Team project (4 analysts, 4 weekly sprints) for a fictional logistics company: from SQL data audit to business recommendations. Logistic and ordinal regression to profile absenteeism risk, a composite 8-KPI performance score, and an analysis of national survey data (EAL 2022–2024) where I led the **training-barriers** workstream with year-on-year Z-tests.
`SQL` `MySQL` `Python` `statsmodels` `Power BI`
🔗 [Repository](https://github.com/vbujaldon/hr-risk-and-training-analytics)
